# Gangtok to Gurudongmar via Lachen & Lachung

A breathtaking journey through North Sikkim, reaching some of the highest and most sacred territories in the state.

## Scenic Landmarks and Viewpoints

### On the Way (Gangtok-Lachung Highway)

- **Seven Sisters Waterfall** - Seven cascading falls harmonizing down rugged cliffs. Best during monsoon.
- **Mangan View Point** - North Sikkim HQ with incredible Kanchenjunga views.
- **Lachung-Lachen Confluence** - Where rivers meet to form the Teesta, near Chungthang.
- **Bheem Nala Fall** - Aka "Amitabh Bachchan Falls" — impressively tall with powerful flow.

### The Lachen Branch (High Altitude & Lakes)

- **Lachen Monastery (1858)** - Traditional Tibetan architecture, colorful murals, village views.
- **Thangu Valley** - Alpine acclimatization stop with wooden houses and nomadic culture.
- **Chopta Valley View Point** - Alpine meadows near Tibet border, meandering rivers.
- **Gurudongmar Lake (17,800 ft)** - One of the world's highest lakes, sacred to multiple faiths.

### The Lachung Branch (Valleys and Flowers)

- **Yumthang Valley** - "Valley of Flowers" with 24+ rhododendron species and hot springs.
- **Zero Point (Yumesamdong, 15,300 ft)** - Last civilian outpost, perpetual snow.
- **Katao** - Off-beat snow sports destination, requires army permits.
- **Lachung Monastery** - Culturally significant, hosts Mask Dance festivals.

## Route Info

- **Total Distance**: ~190 km one way
- **Altitude Range**: 5,500 ft (Gangtok) to 17,800 ft (Gurudongmar)
- **Best Season**: March-June, October-December
- **Permits Required**: Protected Area Permit (PAP) for Indian nationals, Restricted Area Permit (RAP) for foreigners
